{# The kitchen clock: what a loaf underway wants next, and when. Expects `bake`, `view` (services.followalong.NowView, or none for a bake started without a plan) and `now`. A wall clock in the shelf's own pine, its hands set to the time of the next thing to do — not to the time it is. The time it is now is the small dot on the rim, and the band from the dot to the hand is how long there is: read on the minutes when it is under an hour, on the hours beyond. So the picture answers "when?" at a glance from across the kitchen, and the words beside it say what. Drawn here, in the page's own colours for objects, like the jars: cream face and pine rim in either theme.
