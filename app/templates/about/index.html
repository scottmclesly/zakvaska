About zakvaska

Закваска — sourdough starter. A lab notebook for it: jars, feedings, recipes, bakes and how they turned out.

Why it exists

The target is Borodinsky — dark rye, coriander, molasses-black crumb. Getting there means running two rye starters side by side, a homemade whole-rye one against a bought Soviet culture as the control, and keeping enough of a record to tell which jar and which change in the recipe actually made the difference.

Which is why nothing here is free text if it can be a number: feedings carry ratios and rise, recipes carry baker's percentages, and a bake carries the version it came from.

Finding your way round

Jars
One per starter. Log a feeding and the next one is due on the jar's own schedule; the banner at the top of every page is what is overdue. Moving a jar to the fridge or back changes that schedule with it.
Recipes
Stored as baker's percentages against total flour, so the grams fall out of whatever batch size you ask for. Editing a recipe makes a new version rather than overwriting the old one — a loaf you baked in March still reads the way it was baked.
Bakes
Started from a recipe version, with Bake this at the top of that version's page — that is what pins the loaf to the recipe as it stood. The stages become a running sheet you tick off, and the result goes on at the end.
Equipment
The bread maker's programs, typed in from its manual, so a bake can say which one it ran. The 2 / 2.5 / 3 lb buttons on the start form scale the flour to what the pan takes.
Photos
On jars, feedings and bakes. Compare crumb on the bakes page puts two loaves side by side.

How numbers are kept

  • Weights are stored in grams and temperatures in °C — one set of numbers, no rounding drift. The two chips in the header only change what you read: grams, then US cups and spoons, then ounces and pounds.
  • Cup readings come from a density per ingredient and are approximate by nature, so they are marked as such and nothing is ever stored that way. Type weights in grams; that is what the scale says.
  • Times are stored in UTC and shown in {{ tz_name }}.
  • Jars, recipes and bakes archive rather than delete. A notebook you can tear pages out of is not much of a record.
